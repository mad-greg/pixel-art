# Project: Kilchberg (ZH) v10 2×+ Resolution & Peak 90s SVGA Diorama Uplevel

## Architecture
Kilchberg v10 upgrades the static GitHub Pages 2:1 isometric pixel-art diorama (`docs/`) from `0.25 m/px` (`12,331 × 5,444`) to **`0.125 m/px`** (**`~24,662 × 10,888`**, exact 2× linear / 4× pixel area uplevel) with **`0.11 m/px`** high-res museum detail plates, **native 10 cm `SWISSIMAGE DOP10`** roof/ground sampling (`ortho_10cm.npy`), peak mid-90s SVGA pixel-art micro-detail, a **2-level `1024 px` tile pyramid** (`L1 0.25 m/px` + `L2 0.125 m/px`) over a `0.50 m/px` overview (`~6,165 × 2,722`), and `DEPTH_DS = 4` (`~6,165 × 2,722`) depth occlusion preserving all Kilchberg guardrails.

- **Pipeline (`pipeline/`)**:
  - `fetch_data.py` + `extract_scene.py`: Rebuilds ephemeral `/tmp/kilchberg-cache/` and builds mmap-backed **`ortho_10cm.npy`** (`0.10 m/px`, `29600 × 21400 × 3`, `1.77 GiB` on `/tmp` via `np.lib.format.open_memmap`, `< 300 MB` build RAM, `mmap_mode="r"`).
  - `pixel_shader.py`:
    - `0.125 m/px` master geometry (`~24,662 × 10,888`, 2× scaled margins & cartouche), `/tmp` `np.memmap` backing for `full_rgb` & `depth_rgb` (zero full-canvas PIL copy), **`12 × 6` macro-tiles** (`guard_x=48, guard_y=112`), empty-sky skip, streaming `0.50 m/px` overview (`ov_ds = max(2, round(0.50 / mpp))`), 2-level tile pyramid export (`L1` `tiles/{mode}/z1/{c}_{r}.webp` + `L2` `tiles/{mode}/{c}_{r}.webp`), and `DEPTH_DS = max(2, int(round(0.50 / mpp)))` (`4` at `0.125 m/px`, `occ_cnt >= 8` = 50% majority, `is_thin_overhead` exclusions preserved).
    - P0 2× scaling integrity: `0.125 m` (`4×4`) sub-sampling for ground & elevated bridges/viaducts with bilinear interpolation of `0.5 m` continuous fields (`dem`, `bathy_z`, `road_dist`, `road_along`, `road_hw`, `rail_dist`, `rail_along`, `meadow_noise_05m`, `terr_nx/ny/nz`, `dist_lake_05m`); scaled cutaway/hedge splats; 1px micro-hash; all post-process radii (`rx_p`, `rb_h`, `sigma`, `D_MAX_REFL`, Contact AO) scaled by `px_scale = 0.25 / camera.mpp`; live **night Seebad cyan-bleed bug fixed** via exact pool tagging.
    - P1/P2 90s SVGA craft: crisp pitched/flat roofs (keeping Lindt HoC `id=1443` `base_w=0.95` & `is_pv` untouched on native 10 cm DOP10!), 2× multi-pane windows/shutters/sills/lintels/stringcourses/quoins/plinths/warm night window gradients/sunlit bevel + tinted indigo sel-out, organic multi-cluster trees, 4-band bilinear Lake Zurich depth water with Bayer 4×4 dithering & crisp wavelets, warm-sun/cool-shadow contrast, `<= 256` curated SVGA colors.
  - `signage.py`: Sharper baked isometric facade/rooftop lettering (`LINDT HOME OF CHOCOLATE` `thr=0.46` + drop-shadow, crisper `LINDT & SPRÜNGLI`, unclipped `SEEBAD KILCHBERG`, +4 new baked landmark signs: `bendlikon_pier`, `hallenbad_pool_1448`, `stockengut_barn_807`, `tc_clubhouse_1287`) and upgraded 20–30 px clock dials with metallic bezel, cardinal hour bars & authentic SBB red seconds-paddle on `bahnhof_station` (zero floating UI fonts).
  - `render_all.py`: Defaults `mpp_full = 0.125`, `mpp_crop = 0.11`; adds `--macro-rows START END` and `--finalize-tiles` CLI flags so every split command finishes in `~5–7 min` (`< 10 min`) and peak RAM stays `~2.8–3.2 GiB` (of `7.0 GiB` avail, `0 B` swap).
  - `export_animations.py`: Exports `"shift_y": round(float(shift_y), 6)` in `kilchberg_animations.json` (`< 650 KB`, 100% backward-compatible schema).
  - `verify_quality.py`: Updated for v10 (`width >= 23000`, `height >= 10400`, `mpp <= 0.13`, dynamic center SVGA tiles, `macroCols`/`macroRows` seam check, `kilchberg_depth.png` size == `diorama_day.webp` size, `>= 3200 × 2200` detail plates).
- **Frontend (`docs/`)**:
  - `?v=10.0` synced across `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` (`ASSET_VER = '10.0'`), `12.5 cm/px` fidelity display, `0.11 m/px` plate labels.
  - `wScale = (worldW / masterW) * (0.25 / mppMaster)` in `app.js` & `audio.js`; `scale * wScale` LOD checks in `app.js`, `pedestrians.js`, `vignettes.js`, `audio.js`.
  - `depth.js`: reads `animData.shift_y`, scales `extraVertPx` by `0.25 / dc.mppMaster` (100% preserving opaque train & bus roofs).
  - `pedestrians.js` & `traffic.js`: scales all pixel distances/offsets & crosswalk binding (`25.0 * resFactor`) by `resFactor = 0.25 / mppMaster`, and `connLenM` by `mppMaster * 1.12`.
  - `app.js`: 2-level tile pyramid selection (`diorama_{mode}.webp` → `z1` `0.25 m/px` → `L2` `0.125 m/px`) + LRU `tileCache` cap (`MAX_CACHED_TILES = 128`).
  - `vehicles.js`: 2× raster sprite buffers for `zsg_steamer`, `sailboat`, `boesch` divided by `sp.resScale` in `blitWorldSprite`.

## Interface Contracts

### 1. Master Resolution, Tile Pyramid & Manifest (`pipeline/pixel_shader.py` → `docs/assets/tiles/{mode}/manifest.json` → `docs/app.js`)
- `mpp`: `0.125` (`<= 0.125 m/px`); master canvas: `width >= 24000`, `height >= 10600` (target **`24,662 × 10,888`** with 2× scaled margins).
- `tileSize`: `1024`; L2 full-res tiles at `tiles/{mode}/{c}_{r}.webp` (`cols = ceil(width / 1024) = 25`, `rows = ceil(height / 1024) = 11`, `275` tiles/preset, lossless `VP8L`, `<= 256` SVGA colors).
- L1 mid-res pyramid tiles at `tiles/{mode}/z1/{c}_{r}.webp` (`width // 2 × height // 2` = `12,331 × 5,444`, `0.25 m/px`, `13 × 6 = 78` tiles/preset, lossless `VP8L`).
- `manifest.json` includes: `width`, `height`, `mpp`, `tileSize`, `cols`, `rows`, `macroCols` (`12`), `macroRows` (`6`), and `z1: { width, height, mpp: 0.25, tileSize: 1024, cols: 13, rows: 6, path: "z1" }`.
- Overview `docs/assets/diorama_{mode}.webp` & `renders/kilchberg_diorama_{mode}.png`: `ov_ds = max(2, round(0.50 / mpp))` (`~6,165 × 2,722`, `0.50 m/px`, `< 8192 px` GPU safe).
- Detail plates `docs/assets/01..10_*.webp`: `mpp_crop = 0.11 m/px`, lossless `VP8L` RGB, `>= 3200 × 2200`, `<= 256` SVGA colors, zero floating sky clusters.

### 2. Depth Map & Animations Alignment (`pipeline/pixel_shader.py` + `pipeline/export_animations.py` → `docs/js/render/depth.js` + `docs/js/sim/*`)
- `docs/assets/kilchberg_depth.png`: `DEPTH_DS = max(2, int(round(0.50 / mpp)))` (`4` at `0.125 m/px`), dimensions strictly equal to `docs/assets/diorama_day.webp` (`~6,165 × 2,722`).
- Occlusion threshold: `has_occ = occ_cnt >= (DEPTH_DS * DEPTH_DS) // 2` (`>= 8` at `DEPTH_DS = 4`, 50% majority); `is_thin_overhead` exclusions (`310000..310199`, `238000..244999`) preserved verbatim.
- `docs/data/kilchberg_animations.json` (`< 650 KB`): exports `canvas_width`, `canvas_height`, `mpp`, and `"shift_y": round(float(shift_y), 6)`. All existing keys (`trains[2]`, `roads[8]`, `ships[5]`, `network`, `ambient.swiss_flags[4]`, `ambient.vignettes`) preserved 100%.
- `docs/app.js` & `docs/js/audio.js`: `wScale = (worldW / masterW) * (0.25 / mppMaster)`; all LOD visibility thresholds use `scale * wScale`.
- `docs/js/render/depth.js`: reads `animData.shift_y`; scales `extraVertPx` by `0.25 / dc.mppMaster`.
- `docs/js/sim/pedestrians.js` & `docs/js/sim/traffic.js`: scales all `0.25 m/px` pixel thresholds & crosswalk binding by `resFactor = 0.25 / mppMaster`, and `connLenM` by `mppMaster * 1.12`.

### 3. Global Kilchberg Guardrails & Size Budget (Zero Tolerance)
- `docs/` total size `< 300 MB` (well below GitHub Pages 1 GB limit); zero files `> 90 MB`.
- Zero `paraglider`/`hot-air`/`balloon`/`glider` anywhere in `docs/`.
- Slow SUP paddlers (~1.5–1.8 m/s); exact 0.5 m DEM roads edge-to-edge (no off-road drift, no walking on roofs or train tracks).
- Authentic SWISSIMAGE DOP10 satellite roof textures (especially Lindt HoC `id=1443` `base_w=0.95` on native 10 cm DOP10).
- Baked isometric landmark lettering & clocks (`pipeline/signage.py` — zero floating screen-space UI font overlays), 4-petal white-flower Kilchberg municipal flag, Bahnhof NW info board/clock behind passing trains, opaque train & bus roofs, spotless Lake Zurich shoreline, smooth Bendlikon/Dorfstrasse viaduct, flat SBB rail bridges, deterministic simulation, shore-strict 9-channel spatial audio.
- Never output `localhost` URLs. Split long renders strictly `< 10 min` per command.
